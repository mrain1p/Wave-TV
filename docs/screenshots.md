# The screenshots

The six frames are genuine captures of the app on a television, with the
now-playing **content** replaced: the cover is `art/covers/02-spectrum.png`,
original artwork under this repository's MIT licence, and the track, artist,
album and genre metadata are placeholders naming the station's own
programming.

They previously showed a commercially released single with its real cover,
title, artist, album and genre tags. That reproduced artwork this repository
has no licence to, and presented the app as a way to hear commercial music —
which is not what it is for, and is the likeliest thing to have failed
Amazon's content review.

## What was and was not changed

Only content was substituted. No part of the interface, layout, control or
capability was altered, added or invented: what the frames show the app doing
is what the app does. Placeholder content in store screenshots is ordinary
practice — the app displays whatever the connected station provides, so no one
track is more representative than another — but the substitution is recorded
here so the position can be stated plainly if a store ever asks.

## Replacing them

Best is a fresh capture against a station playing material its operator holds
the rights to: original programming, a talk or mix show, or explicitly
licensed or public-domain music. Then nothing needs substituting at all.

If a frame would show a record sleeve you did not make and cannot license, it
is the wrong frame.

Capture at **1920×1080** on a real television.

## Where they go

- `store/amazon/screenshots/` and `store/play/screenshots/` — the same six
  PNGs; both stores take an identical set.
- `docs/` — the same frames as JPG (`picker-light.jpg`, `picker-dark.jpg`,
  `picker-minimal.jpg`, `player.jpg`, `request.jpg`, `keyboard.jpg`) for the
  README table.

Screenshots must be genuine captures of the running app. Do not mock the
interface up or invent a control that does not exist.
